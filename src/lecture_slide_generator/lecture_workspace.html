Create a presentation from a topic

Enter the subject and select your audience. Qwen will generate the teaching content.

Topic generation produces a PowerPoint directly. PDF generation offers an outline review before export.

1. Upload lecture materials

Text-based PDFs. Current limits: 300 pages total, 240,000 extracted characters, 65 text sections. No OCR yet.

Large uploads require several Qwen calls; the browser must remain open while processing. Only use materials you have permission to process.

2. Review and edit the teaching sequence

Edit slide titles, adjust slide order, and revise source-backed teaching points. Each source must remain represented. Page references are approximate source-location aids and should be checked.
